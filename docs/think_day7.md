# Think Day 7 - Attrition Privacy

Question: A department has only 4 people. What do you show, and why?

Answer:

I would hide the number. I will show "Hidden (<5)".

Because 4 people is very small team. If I write 1 person high risk, then other 3 will easily guess who it is. That is not good. Person will feel bad and privacy will be gone.

In class also we discussed, prediction is not final decision. It is just to help HR to talk to team. So we should not label small teams.

Thats why I added rule in my code - if less than 5 people, hide it. I only show department wise like 58 people in Support, 20 high risk. No names.

So for 4 people team, better to hide than show.
