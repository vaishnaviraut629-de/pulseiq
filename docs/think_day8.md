### Think Day 8: Team A has best velocity but most reopened. Which team is healthier?

Honestly, I'd go with Team B - the one with lower velocity but less reopened.

Here's how I think about it:

Team A looks great on paper - say 180 tasks per sprint. But if 22% of that is reopened, that's around 40 tasks coming back. So your actual clean delivery is only ~140. And those 40 reopened tasks are worse than normal tasks. They create context switching, customer frustration, and you end up fixing the same thing twice.

Team B is doing 120 tasks with only 5% reopened. So they actually deliver ~114 solid tasks. No noise, no rework. Customers are happier, the team is less stressed, and what they ship stays shipped.

For me, health is not just speed. It's sustainable speed + quality.

A team with high reopened usually has underlying issues:
- No proper code reviews
- Too much WIP, rushing to close
- Tech debt piling up
- Maybe testing is skipped to hit velocity numbers

So Team A is fast but fragile. Team B is slower but stable.

My decision: I'd pick Team B as healthier any day. 

If I had to fix Team A, I'd tell them to slow down a bit, add proper QA, limit WIP, and track reopened rate as a first-class metric, not just velocity.

At the end, a healthy team = does good work that doesn't come back.
